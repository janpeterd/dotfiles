The settlement journal supports one transfer and atomic ordered batches. Exact retries debit once, conflicting ids fail, owner epochs fence late requests, and invalid items or generator failures roll back new receipts and balances in that batch. Receipts and balances share one SQLite connection. There is no requirement for separate storage implementations, concurrent worker threads or external transactions.
